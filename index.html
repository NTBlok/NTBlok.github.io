Description

Role-play with AI in casual conversations like meeting friends at a cafe or discussing movies with a friend. Alternatively, role-play in more formal roles such as speaking with a customer service representative or a store clerk.

Privacy Policy

Convo-Sesh is designed with your complete privacy in mind.


No Data Collection

  • Convo-Sesh does NOT collect any user information
  • No user data is transmitted to any servers
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No personal information is stored or shared

Local-Only Storage

  • All conversations are local to your device
  • Vocabulary lists are saved locally to your phone
  • App settings and preferences remain on your device
  • No internet connection required for core functionality

Complete Privacy

  • No app backend or cloud infrastructure
  • AI processing happens locally on your device
  • Your conversations never leave your phone
  • You have full control over your data
